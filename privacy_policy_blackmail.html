BLACKMAIL Sakari Games

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

In short: Blackmail saves your progress in an anonymous game account, without your name, e-mail or password. The game is free and shows ads through Unity; Unity receives technical data from your phone to deliver and count them, and ads are only personalised if you agree. Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store, so we never see your payment details.

Who we are

Blackmail is a sniper game for Android and iPhone made by Sakari Games, a game studio based in Antwerp, Belgium. Sakari Games is responsible for the game and for this policy. You can reach us at info@sakari.be.

Your game account

To keep your progress, the game creates an anonymous player account at PlayFab, a game backend of Microsoft, the first time you play. You do not sign up and we never ask for your name, e-mail address or a password. The account holds:

  • an identifier of your device (Android ID or Apple's identifier for vendors) and the device model and operating system, so the game finds your account again;
  • your progress: completed missions, money, hints, weapon parts and experience;
  • your scores on the leaderboards, and the player name you choose if you enter one (it is shown to other players there);
  • receipts of purchases, so the game can check them with Google or Apple and give you what you bought.

Microsoft processes this data for us under its privacy statement. We keep it as long as you play; ask us and we delete your account and everything in it.

Advertising

Blackmail is free and shows ads: short ads between missions and video ads you choose to watch for a reward. They come through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies, which fills them from Unity Ads and ironSource Ads (also Unity). To deliver an ad, make sure it is shown and paid for, and prevent fraud, Unity receives technical data from your phone, such as:

  • your IP address and a rough location derived from it (country or region, not your exact position);
  • the phone model, operating system version, language and network type;
  • your phone's advertising identifier (Google's advertising ID on Android; on iPhone Apple's IDFA, only if you allow tracking);
  • which ads were shown to you, whether you watched them to the end and whether you tapped them.

Your choice. The game asks whether our ad partners may use data from your phone to show you ads that suit you. On iPhone, iOS also asks whether Blackmail may track you across apps and websites. Ads are only personalised if you agree; if you do not, you get the same number of ads, just not based on your interests. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings > Privacy > Ads; on iPhone you can switch tracking off under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Sakari Games only receives totals from Unity, such as the number of ads shown and what they earned, which do not identify individual players.

Purchases

You can buy items in the game, such as hints. Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store under their own privacy policies; Sakari Games never receives your payment details, only a receipt that the purchase was made.

Google, Apple and the stores

You get Blackmail through Google Play or the App Store. Google and Apple handle your accounts, downloads and the crash reports you choose to share, under their own privacy policies (Google, Apple). They show us totals such as downloads and crash rates, which do not identify individual players.

Children

Blackmail is a shooting game for teenagers and adults (see the age rating in the store) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has played and you want its account removed, contact us.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Mail info@sakari.be and we will answer within a month; to find your account we may ask for the player name or a screenshot of your progress. For data Unity received to show you ads, you can use the requests described in Unity's privacy policy. If you believe your data is being handled wrongly, you can also contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Changes to this policy

If an update changes what the game collects or which partners are involved, we update this page before that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest version.
