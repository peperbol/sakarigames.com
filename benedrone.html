BeNeDrone is an international collaboration between Belgium and The Netherlands. Bringing together multiple partners from different fields such as Sakari for virtual development.

This project explores how drones can be used efficiently, safely, and legally in cross-border medical transport. We are mapping out the medical and economic benefits, studying regulations, and building virtual environments where professionals and policymakers can experience the results first hand.

At Sakari we will be creating the visualisation combining the studies data and showing it off in a clear immersive virtual environment.
