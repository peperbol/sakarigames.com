Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: SQUIDS does not collect, store or share any personal data. The only thing the game saves is your best distance, and that stays on your headset.

Who we are

SQUIDS is a motorcycle game for Meta Quest made by Sakari Games, a game studio based in Antwerp, Belgium. Sakari Games is responsible for the game and for this policy. You can reach us at info@sakari.be.

What the game collects

Nothing that identifies you. SQUIDS has no accounts, no online features, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use the internet to play.

  • Best distance. Your longest ride is saved on the headset so the game can show it next time. It is never sent anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • Head and controller movement. The game reads the position of your headset and controllers while you play so you can lean to steer and tuck in for speed. This happens on the headset in real time and is not recorded or stored.

The game does not use your microphone, cameras, passthrough, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Meta and the Meta Horizon Store

You get SQUIDS through the Meta Horizon Store, and it runs on Meta Horizon OS. Meta handles your Meta account, downloads, purchases, payment details and system-level crash and performance reports under its own Meta Quest privacy policy. Sakari Games does not receive your payment details or account information. Through the developer dashboard Meta shows us totals such as number of downloads, sales and crash rates, which do not identify individual players.

Children

SQUIDS is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal data from anyone, it also collects none from children. Meta Quest accounts for younger players are managed by Meta and by parents through Meta's own parental tools.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. For SQUIDS we hold none, but you are always welcome to ask at info@sakari.be. If you believe your data is being handled wrongly, you can also contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Changes to this policy

If a future update adds something that touches personal data, for example online leaderboards, in-app purchases or advertising, we will update this page before that version is released and say exactly what is collected, why, and which partners are involved. The date at the top shows the latest version.
