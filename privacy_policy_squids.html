SQUIDS Sakari Games

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

In short: on Meta Quest, SQUIDS does not collect, store or share any personal data. On iPhone, the game itself collects nothing either, but it can show optional video ads (you watch one when you want extra credits). Our ad partner, Unity, then receives some technical data from your phone to deliver and count those ads. Ads are only personalised if you say yes, both to our question in the game and to Apple's tracking prompt.

Who we are

SQUIDS is a motorcycle game for Meta Quest and iPhone made by Sakari Games, a game studio based in Antwerp, Belgium. Sakari Games is responsible for the game and for this policy. You can reach us at info@sakari.be.

What the game collects

Nothing that identifies you. SQUIDS has no accounts, no online features of its own and no analytics. On Meta Quest it does not use the internet at all.

  • Best distance and credits. Your longest ride, and on iPhone your number of credits, are saved on your device so the game can show them next time. They are never sent anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • Movement. On Meta Quest the game reads the position of your headset and controllers so you can lean to steer and tuck in for speed. On iPhone it reads the phone's motion sensors so you can tilt it like handlebars. This happens on the device in real time and is not recorded or stored.

The game does not use your microphone, cameras, passthrough, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Advertising (iPhone only)

SQUIDS on iPhone is free. After a crash you can wait a few seconds before you ride on, or use a credit to ride on straight away. You earn credits by beating your best distance or by watching a short video ad, which is always your choice: the game never plays an ad you did not ask for.

The ads come through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies, which fills them from Unity Ads and ironSource Ads (also Unity). To deliver an ad, make sure it is shown and paid for, and prevent fraud, Unity receives technical data from your phone, such as:

  • your IP address and a rough location derived from it (country or region, not your exact position);
  • the phone model, iOS version, language and network type;
  • Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV), and Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • which ads were shown to you, whether you watched them to the end and whether you tapped them.

Your choice. The first time you start the game it asks whether our ad partners may use data from your phone to show you ads that suit you. If you say yes, iOS then asks whether SQUIDS may track you across apps and websites. Only when you agree to both can ads be personalised. If you say no to either, you still get the same ads and credits, but they are not based on your interests. You can change your answer in the game at any time with the Privacy button on the start screen, and switch tracking off under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone.

Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Sakari Games only receives totals from Unity, such as the number of ads shown and what they earned, which do not identify individual players.

Apple and the App Store

On iPhone you get SQUIDS through the App Store. Apple handles your Apple Account, downloads and crash reports you choose to share, under its own privacy policy. Through App Store Connect Apple shows us totals such as downloads and crash rates, which do not identify individual players.

Meta and the Meta Horizon Store

You get SQUIDS through the Meta Horizon Store, and it runs on Meta Horizon OS. Meta handles your Meta account, downloads, purchases, payment details and system-level crash and performance reports under its own Meta Quest privacy policy. Sakari Games does not receive your payment details or account information. Through the developer dashboard Meta shows us totals such as number of downloads, sales and crash rates, which do not identify individual players.

Children

The game's content is suitable for all ages, but SQUIDS is not directed at children under 13. The Quest version collects no personal data from anyone, children included. On iPhone, the game asks no personal questions; ads shown to players who do not agree to personalisation are not based on their interests. Meta Quest and Apple accounts for younger players are managed by Meta, Apple and parents through their own parental tools.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Sakari Games itself holds no personal data from SQUIDS, but you are always welcome to ask at info@sakari.be. For data Unity received to show you ads, you can use the requests described in Unity's privacy policy. If you believe your data is being handled wrongly, you can also contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Changes to this policy

If a future update adds something else that touches personal data, for example online leaderboards or in-app purchases, we will update this page before that version is released and say exactly what is collected, why, and which partners are involved. The date at the top shows the latest version.
